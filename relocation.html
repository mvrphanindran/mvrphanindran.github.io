For units on the move

Relocation Visibility Kit

Move your plant without losing a single enquiry.

Many Hyderabad manufacturers are moving out of the city's older industrial estates to sites beyond the ORR. When a unit moves, the old address stays on Google, IndiaMART, the website and every directory for months. Buyers and drivers arrive at the wrong gate, and enquiries go to a number nobody answers. This kit moves your presence with you.

  1. Every listing updatedGoogle Business Profile, IndiaMART, TradeIndia, JustDial and LinkedIn, with the new address, map pin and hours
  2. Website updatedAddress, map, contact details and a short "we have moved" notice on every page
  3. Customers and suppliers informedA professional announcement by email and WhatsApp, with directions to the new plant
  4. New plant, shown wellA photo set and a short video of the new facility for your website, LinkedIn and Google
  5. Nothing left behindA check of old listings and links, so the old address stops appearing
